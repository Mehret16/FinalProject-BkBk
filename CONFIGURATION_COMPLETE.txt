THE THERAPIST REFERRAL SYSTEM (YOUR MAIN FEATURE)
================================================================================

WORKFLOW:

Patient creates referral request
           ↓
POST /api/referrals/create
           ↓
Backend generates JWT access token (7-day expiry)
           ↓
Email automatically sent to therapist:
  "Click to access patient profile and history"
           ↓
Therapist clicks email link
           ↓
Frontend receives access_token from email
           ↓
Frontend sends to backend:
  POST /api/referrals/access?access_token=JWT_TOKEN
           ↓
Backend verifies token & returns:
  - Complete patient profile
  - All chat sessions
  - Complete message history
  - Referral details
  - Sentiment analysis data
           ↓
Therapist has full access to patient data

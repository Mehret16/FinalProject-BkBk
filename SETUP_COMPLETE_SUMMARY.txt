HOW IT WORKS:

Patient clicks "Request Therapist"
    ↓
Backend generates secure JWT token
    ↓
Email automatically sent to therapist with:
  "Click to access patient profile and history"
    ↓
Therapist clicks link in email
    ↓
Therapist gets FULL access to:
  • Patient profile & information
  • All chat sessions
  • Complete message history
  • Referral details
  • Sentiment analysis

  See PROJECT_SUMMARY.md → Therapist Referral System section

  POST /api/referrals/create .............. (Create referral)
  POST /api/referrals/access ............. (Therapist accesses data)
